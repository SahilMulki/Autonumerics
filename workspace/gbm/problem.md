# Geometric Brownian Motion

Simulate the following stochastic differential equation (Itô):

    dX = 0.1 X dt + 0.2 X dW,   X(0) = 1.0

over the time interval [0, 1].

Compute the empirical mean E[X(T)] and variance Var[X(T)] at T = 1 using Monte Carlo simulation and compare to the known analytic moments.
